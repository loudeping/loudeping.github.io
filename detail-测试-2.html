《茶道》 The Way of Tea
Drain the morning dew, drink the sunset clouds dry — into the old pot, another handful of stars. Hand never leaves the pot; feel how vast heaven and earth are. Lips never leave the tea; ask who has enlightened the years. 饮尽晨露，喝断晚霞，老壶内又放进星星一把。 手不离壶，摸一摸乾坤有多大。 口不离茶，问一问岁月谁点化。
⠿
